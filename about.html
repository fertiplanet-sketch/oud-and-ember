    About Us

    Oud & Ember began with a simple idea: the scents of the Gulf — oud smoke, Taif rose, saffron in coffee — deserve to be crafted slowly and shared honestly.

    Our story

    Founded in Dubai in 2026, Oud & Ember is a small perfume house built around the ingredients of the Arabian Peninsula. We work in small batches, so every bottle can be checked by hand.

    We are inspired by majlis evenings, desert sunsets and the old trading routes that carried frankincense across the region — and we try to make perfume that feels personal, not mass-produced.

    What we stand for

    • Slow craft: every blend rests for six weeks before bottling.
    • Honest ingredients: we name every key note and where it comes from.
    • Respect for heritage: traditional materials, presented for today.
    • Transparent AI: we label AI-generated content and never use it to invent customers, reviews or people.

    Perfume, slow-crafted in the Emirates.
