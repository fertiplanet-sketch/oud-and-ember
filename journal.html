    Heritage · October 2026

    Oud: the wood of the Gulf

    Long before it appeared in perfume bottles, oud was burned on charcoal to scent homes, clothes and hair. Its smoke still marks weddings, Eid and the welcome of a guest.

    Our Midnight Oud keeps that smoky depth but softens it with vanilla, so it is easy to wear every day.

    Guide · September 2026

    Layering perfume: a beginner’s guide

    Layering means wearing two scents together to make something personal. Start with the heavier scent first — try Midnight Oud on the wrists — then add a lighter one such as Jasmine Majlis on top.

    Keep it to two perfumes, and test on a blotter strip before your skin.
