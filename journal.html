    Behind the scenes · November 2026

    Smart operations: how a perfume is made

    Every Oud & Ember perfume starts as a mood board of colours, textures and memories. From there, one simple digital plan follows each batch from raw ingredient to sealed bottle.

    1. Brief: the mood board becomes a scent brief with three to five key notes.
    2. Sourcing: each ingredient is logged with its origin and arrival date.
    3. Blending: batches of 200 bottles or fewer, each with its own batch sheet.
    4. Resting: six weeks, with scheduled smell checks along the way.
    5. Bottling: filled and sealed by hand, every bottle traceable to its batch.

    Planning this way means we order only what we need, waste less, and can answer any question about a bottle in seconds.

    Heritage · October 2026

    Oud: the wood of the Gulf

    Long before it appeared in perfume bottles, oud was burned on charcoal to scent homes, clothes and hair. Its smoke still marks weddings, Eid and the welcome of a guest.

    Our Midnight Oud keeps that smoky depth but softens it with vanilla, so it is easy to wear every day.

    Guide · September 2026

    Layering perfume: a beginner’s guide

    Layering means wearing two scents together to make something personal. Start with the heavier scent first — try Midnight Oud on the wrists — then add a lighter one such as Jasmine Majlis on top.

    Keep it to two perfumes, and test on a blotter strip before your skin.
